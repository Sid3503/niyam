### Proposed new rule card ids
- `NPCI-CHARGEBACK-WINDOW`: customer chargeback can be raised up to 45 days (OC 198); agent must not give another window.
- `NPCI-UDIR-UPIHELP-ROUTE`: for a pending/failed UPI txn, agent directs to bank app/UPI Help raising a complaint with a CRN, and does not require customer to reconcile (CC para 32-33).
- `NPCI-UPI-CHARGES`: no surcharge to users; convenience fee disclosed before payment-mode selection (CC 7.5); MDR on select P2M is merchant-side and pending (press note).
- `NPCI-UPI-LIMITS`: do not misstate P2P/merchant limits (OC 185B).
- `IRDAI-MISSELL-DEF`, `IRDAI-AD-MISLEADING`, `IRDAI-FREELOOK` (30 days, refund within 7 days), `IRDAI-GRIEVANCE-TAT` (ack immediately, 14 days), `IRDAI-OMB-ESCALATION` (up to Rs 50 lakh, award in 30 days), `IRDAI-ILLUSTRATION-GUARANTEED` (guaranteed vs non-guaranteed, 4%/8%).
- `PFRDA-GRIEVANCE-30D`.

## 4. Corrections to NIYAM's existing claims (from the primary text)

1. **RBI-TAT-UPI-P2M is wrong as written.** NIYAM says a failed P2M transaction must be auto-reversed within T+1. RBI annex row 4b says: "payment to merchant" where confirmation is not received - auto-reversal within **T+5**, Rs 100/day beyond T+5. T+1 applies only to row 4a (transfer of funds, beneficiary not credited).
2. **RBI-TAT-UPI-P2P wording.** The T+1 reversal is by the **Beneficiary bank** ("If unable to credit the beneficiary account, auto reversal (R) by the Beneficiary bank latest on T + 1 day"), not the originating bank as the card says. Compensation is Rs 100/day beyond T+1, payable suo moto (para 5).
3. **Does NPCI define P2P vs P2M differently for reversal?** No separate NPCI reversal TAT exists in what I could read. The NPCI Consolidated Circular (para 31) points to the RBI TAT framework. The P2P/P2M distinction comes from RBI rows 4a/4b ("transfer of funds" vs "payment to merchant"). NPCI's own taxonomy has P2P, P2M, and P2PM (person-to-person-merchant, MCC 7407) as separate transaction types (CC para 6-7 area); RBI's table does not mention P2PM, so how P2PM maps to 4a vs 4b is unresolved (INFERENCE: not settled by either text).
4. **Rs 100/day** is confirmed (RBI row text) but is per day of delay *beyond the row's TAT*, and ATM/card/PoS rows use T+5 (some T+1); one number does not fit all rows. "TAT is the outer limit" (para 4).
5. **"UDIR / NPCI OC auto-reversal TAT":** UDIR is a dispute-and-pending-transaction automation (para 32), not a source of a separate reversal deadline. The 45-day figure (OC 198) is the customer's chargeback window, not a reversal TAT.
6. **Ombudsman 30-day rule:** RBI 2019 para 6 only sends unresolved customers to the RB Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2021; no 30-day figure appears in it. The 30-day figures I read are IRDAI (insurer must comply with Insurance Ombudsman award in 30 days, Rs 5,000/day penalty) and PFRDA (grievance resolved within 30 days). Do not transfer one regulator's 30 days to another.
7. **UPI Help is itself an AI agent** ("powered by a proprietary financial domain-specific language model", CC para 33). Worth adding to NIYAM's narrative: the payments network already runs a regulated-flow AI support channel, and banks must consume its complaints within TAT.
8. FREE-AI, KFS and the FY2024-25 complaint count are outside this scope and unverified here.
