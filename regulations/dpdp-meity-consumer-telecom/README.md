## 3. Superseded or uncertain status

Act-section staging (secondary sources: judicio.ai, DLA Piper, SCC Online snippets, not G.S.R. 843(E) itself): Board-related provisions immediate; s.6(9) (Consent Manager registration) and s.27(1)(d) at 12 months; remaining substantive obligations including **ss.5, 6, 8, 9 and the penalty schedule at 18 months**. Sources differ on exact section lists and one says 14 May 2027 versus 13 May 2027 - verify against G.S.R. 843(E). Consequence for NIYAM: DPDP-CONSENT-PURPOSE should be marked "effective 2027-05-13 (pending verification)" and run as a forward-looking/ready test now.
- **Penalty schedule (Act, Schedule, read from the extracted table, rows in order)**: up to Rs 250 crore for failure of reasonable security safeguards (s.8(5)); up to Rs 200 crore for failure to notify breach to Board/Data Principals (s.8(6)); up to Rs 200 crore for children's obligations (s.9); up to Rs 150 crore for Significant Data Fiduciary obligations (s.10); up to Rs 10,000 for Data Principal duties (s.15); up to the applicable extent for breach of a voluntary undertaking (s.32); up to Rs 50 crore for any other breach. ## 4. Corrections to NIYAM's existing claims

- **DPDP-CONSENT-PURPOSE wording** ("withdrawal must be as easy as giving"): the Act s.6(4) says the ease of withdrawing must be "**comparable to**" the ease of giving; Rule 3(c)(i) repeats "comparable". Use "comparable", not "as easy as", in the rule card. Also the card cites "plain-language notice of purpose": s.5(1) requires the notice to cover personal data and purpose AND how to exercise s.6(4)/s.13 rights AND how to complain to the Board; s.5(3)/s.6(3) add the English/Eighth Schedule language option; Rule 3 adds an itemised data list and a link/other means for withdrawal, rights and complaints.
- **Effective date**: the card is not currently enforceable (s.5/s.6 and Rules 3, 5-16 commence 13 May 2027 per Rules' text). Do not describe DPDP consent obligations as presently in force.
- **Breach timing**: Rule 7 says 72 hours to the Board for the detailed report, with an initial description "without delay"; CERT-In's separate 6-hour rule applies to listed incident types. These are two different clocks.
- No primary-source check was possible here of FREE-AI, TAT table, Rs 100/day, ombudsman 30-day rule or KFS (outside my scope).
