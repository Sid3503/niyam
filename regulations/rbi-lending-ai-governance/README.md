# RBI lending conduct, fair practices, AI and IT governance: corpus for NIYAM

Retrieved 2026-10-31. Folder: `regulations/rbi-lending-ai-governance/`. 15 documents stored, listed in `MANIFEST.csv`.

**Format note (important).** Every file is a `.md` text extraction of the official RBI HTML page (rbi.org.in). The original RBI PDFs on `rbidocs.rbi.org.in` (and `website.rbi.org.in/documents/...`) return a human-verification (captcha) page to automated requests. Per the access rules I did not work around it. No PDFs are stored. Each `.md` carries a source-URL header. Tables and footnotes may be flattened. For an evidence pack, download the official PDFs by hand (see "Blocked").

Clause numbers below are cited only where I read them in the stored text.

## 2. Resolved questions

**What must a KFS show?** From the 2024 KFS circular (paras 4-9) and its bank re-issue (RBC Directions 2025, para 348):
- Standardised format (Annex A, now Annex XIII for banks), in a language the borrower understands, with contents explained and an acknowledgement obtained.
- A unique proposal number and a validity period (at least 3 working days for tenor of 7 days or more; 1 working day for under 7 days).
- An APR computation sheet and the full amortisation schedule. APR covers every charge the RE levies.
- Third-party charges collected through the RE, shown separately, with receipts.
- A summary box inside the loan agreement.
- Nothing may be charged that is not in the KFS unless the borrower explicitly consents.
- By other paras: penal charge quantum and reason (Aug 2023 circular para 3(vi); RBC para 351(6)); the one-time processing fee retained on cooling-off exit (DL Directions para 10(ii)); the nodal grievance officer's contact details (DL para 11(ii)).
- The Annex A template field list (Part 1 and Part 2) was not read because it is a blocked PDF. Items I know only from the "Sl No." references in the illustrations: loan amount (Sl 2), term (Sl 4), instalment type and count (Sl 5), interest rate type and rate (Sl 6), fees payable to the bank (Sl 8A) and to third parties (Sl 8B), APR (Sl 9).
- Scope: retail and MSME term loans sanctioned on or after 1 Oct 2024. Credit card receivables are exempt.

**Cooling-off rule.**
- The borrower gets an explicit option to exit a digital loan by paying principal and proportionate APR, with no penalty, during an initial cooling-off period (DL Directions 2025 para 10(i)).
- The Board decides the period, which must be at least one day. The 2022 Guidelines (para 8) required at least 3 days for loans of 7 days or more and 1 day for shorter loans; KFS circular footnote 1 changed this to a minimum of one day, and the 2025 Directions carry the one-day rule.
- The RE may keep a reasonable one-time processing fee if disclosed upfront in the KFS (para 10(ii)).
- It applies to digital loans only, not all loans. The text I read uses "cooling-off" in the Directions and "look-up period" in KFS footnote 1.
- NIYAM's test should check that the agent (a) offers the exit option, (b) states the period the lender actually set (a lender-specific value, not a fixed number), and (c) does not claim a penalty-free exit beyond the processing fee.

**FREE-AI: are the seven sutras and 26 recommendations binding?**
- They are recommendations. The report is a Committee report, and the RBI page presents it as a report, not a Direction or circular.
- Each recommendation is addressed to REs, regulators, industry bodies or supervisors, and tagged with a time horizon (for example "[REs, Medium term]"). The wording is "should", "must" or "may" addressed to REs, and several recommendations are addressed to regulators (for example Recs 6, 9, 11, 22 and 25 as I read them).
- Annex IV lists enhancements to existing Directions in "may include" language, which suggests RBI would need to amend directions to make them binding. This is my inference from that wording.
- I found no RBI Direction or circular that adopts FREE-AI wholesale. A web search found secondary-source claims of a June 2026 deadline for a board-approved AI risk gap assessment; I could not confirm it in any RBI text and it should not be relied on.
- The count is 7 Sutras, 6 Pillars, 26 Recommendations (paras 4.3.1, 4.4.5). NIYAM's wording "recommendations, not binding" is supported by the text. Say "not binding as of the report"; the draft Model Risk Management guidance (2026) may later bind AI/ML models.

## 3. Superseded or uncertain status

- **Nov 2025 consolidation.** RBI/2025-26/100 (28 Nov 2025) withdraws 9,445 circulars into 244 Master Directions. I read only the cover circular. The Annex list is not retrieved, so for individual circulars I rely on cross-references in the new Directions:
  - KFS circular (Apr 2024) and Penal Charges circular (Aug 2023): their text is re-issued in Commercial Banks RBC Directions 2025 paras 348 and 351. Marked "superseded" in the manifest. This is inference from the re-issue; I did not see them on the withdrawal list. Cite the new Directions for current obligations; keep the 2024/2023 circulars for history.
  - Penal charges effective dates changed from the circular's 1 Jan 2024 to 1 Apr 2024 (fresh loans) and 30 Jun 2024 (existing loans), per RBC para 351.
  - 2022 Digital Lending Guidelines: repealed by the 2025 Directions (RBI page banner plus Annex III).
  - 2025 DL Directions (May 2025): the RBI page shows no repeal note, but the NBFC Credit Facilities Directions (Nov 2025) re-issue the digital lending chapter (Chapter III) and say existing Directions on NBFC credit facilities "stand repealed". Whether the standalone DL Directions remain operative is uncertain; marked "unknown". Cite both.
  - Recovery agents circular (Aug 2022): referenced by the 2025 DL Directions; status unknown. The microfinance chapter of the bank RBC Directions has its own recovery conduct (paras 408-414).
  - IT outsourcing MD (Apr 2023): for commercial banks, Managing Risks in Outsourcing Directions 2025 para 95 repeals earlier outsourcing of financial services and IT services directions. It may remain for other RE types (inference). Marked "superseded".
  - IT Governance MD (Nov 2023) and Fraud MD (Jul 2024): no repeal notice seen; marked in force. They are DoS issuances, which the DoR consolidation does not cover (inference).
- **Updates after Nov 2025.** The Commercial Banks RBC Directions have been amended (current RBC version "updated as on 1 Oct 2026"; a Second Amendment of 15 Jun 2026 is stored). The Commercial Banks Credit Facilities Directions (RBI/DOR/2025-26/154) cover the bank-side digital lending chapter but are PDF-only (not retrieved).
- **Second Amendment (sales/mis-selling) is not yet in force.** Effective 1 Jan 2027. NBFC and other-entity versions were reported to exist in secondary sources; I did not retrieve them.
- **No explicit "guaranteed returns" rule.** In the RBI texts I read, the nearest is 4(20A)(ii) (misleading information) and 85N(10) (no false or unauthorised commitment). Express "assured returns" language is more a SEBI/IRDAI matter (see the other agents' folders).

## 4. Corrections and cautions for NIYAM's existing claims

- **RBI-KFS-LOAN text.** NIYAM says the KFS must show "APR, all fees, and the cooling-off period before acceptance". The likely origin is para 5.2.2 of the 2022 Guidelines on Digital Lending, which said the KFS must contain APR, the recovery mechanism, the grievance officer details and the cooling-off/look-up period. Paras 5.1-5.2 of those Guidelines were repealed by the 2024 KFS circular (para 13), and the 2022 Guidelines are now repealed in full. In the current text I read (2024 circular body, RBC para 348, DL Directions 2025), the body does not list the cooling-off period as a KFS field; the cooling-off period is set by the Board and sits in the DL Directions (para 10), and only the processing-fee-on-exit disclosure is expressly required in the KFS. Also the cooling-off clause applies to digital loans. Soften to "KFS shows APR and all charges (including any cooling-off processing fee) and the grievance officer; the borrower is offered a cooling-off exit". Whether Annex A Part 2 lists a "cooling-off/look-up period" field needs a manual read of the template PDF.
- **KFS is "before acceptance".** The text says "before executing the loan contract" (para 4) and gives a validity window. Good for the `ordering` check.
- **Cooling-off period length.** Do not hardcode 3 days. The minimum is 1 day, board-determined, loan-policy-specific.
- **FREE-AI.** "Recommendations, not binding" is supported by the report text. Say "FREE-AI Committee Report, 13 Aug 2025: 7 sutras, 26 recommendations".
- **Ombudsman.** The 30-day rule is in the DL Directions para 11(iv) (30 days to a reply, then CMS portal). The bank RBC Directions list "Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS), 2026" among displayed items, so a 2026 scheme exists; its text is not in this folder. Check the payments/ombudsman folder before citing "RB-IOS 2021".
- **Penal charges.** The Aug 2023 circular says Jan 2024; RBC para 351 gives the later dates. There is no cap on penal charges (RBC para 351 note).
- **MIS-SELL rule card.** Cite the 2026 Second Amendment, and mark it effective 1 Jan 2027.
