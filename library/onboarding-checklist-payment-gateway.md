# Merchant onboarding checklist: Payment Gateway

Synthetic demo document for the Niyam prototype.

1. Collect the business PAN, GSTIN where applicable and a bank account in the business's name.

2. Complete KYC and risk verification before activation.

3. Settlements follow the T+2 business-day cycle in the Merchant Agreement.

4. Explain that approval is not guaranteed and activation usually takes 3-5 working days after complete documents.
