# Follow-up after the merchant call

Synthetic demo script for the Niyam prototype. Sales reps adapt it for WhatsApp.

1. Thanks for your time today. As discussed, activation needs your business PAN, GSTIN where applicable, and a bank account in your business name, and it is subject to KYC and risk verification.

2. Settlements follow the T+2 business-day cycle in your Merchant Agreement.

3. Our fees are listed in the fee schedule, and UPI payments carry no MDR.

4. Just so you know, there are no hidden charges anywhere, so you will never see a deduction you did not expect.

5. If you have any complaint, our grievance redressal officer is named in your Merchant Agreement.
