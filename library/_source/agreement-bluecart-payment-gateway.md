# Merchant Agreement: Payment Gateway

Synthetic demo document for the Niyam prototype.

## 1. Services

1.1 Paytm provides the Payment Gateway service to the Merchant on the terms of this Agreement.

## 2. Fees

2.1 Domestic credit and debit card transactions are charged an MDR of 1.90% plus applicable GST.

2.2 UPI transactions carry an MDR of 0.3% payable by the Merchant.

## 3. Settlement

3.1 Settlements are processed on a T+3 cycle from the transaction date.

3.2 Settlement is never held or delayed by Paytm once a transaction succeeds.

## 4. Refunds

4.1 Where a UPI merchant payment fails and the customer's account is debited, the reversal reaches the customer within 5 working days.

## 5. General

5.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.
